Hamkke Teacher Agreement
Hamkke │ 함께
From Small Talk to Big Ideas.

This Teacher Agreement ("Agreement") establishes the terms and
expectations governing the teaching relationship between Hamkke │ 함께
("Hamkke") and the teacher identified in this Agreement ("Teacher").

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish clear expectations
regarding teaching responsibilities, lesson assignments, compensation,
attendance, professional conduct, learner information, teaching
materials, and the responsibilities of both Hamkke and the Teacher.

1. Teaching Role and Responsibilities
The Teacher agrees to provide online English lessons assigned and
confirmed through Hamkke and to carry out teaching responsibilities
professionally, responsibly, and in a manner consistent with Hamkke's
educational standards.

The Teacher is expected to:

prepare appropriately for scheduled lessons;
begin and end lessons on time;
maintain a suitable teaching environment;
maintain reasonably reliable equipment and internet access necessary
for online teaching;
communicate professionally with learners, parents or guardians, and
Hamkke;
accurately update required attendance and lesson records;
complete required feedback, teacher notes, or Progress Reports when
applicable; and
follow applicable Hamkke teaching standards and policies.
The Teacher agrees to provide lessons in accordance with the Hamkke
Teaching Framework and other applicable teaching standards
communicated by Hamkke.

The Hamkke Teaching Framework establishes a shared educational approach
but is not a fixed lesson script. Teachers retain appropriate
professional judgment in adapting instruction to the learner's age,
English level, confidence, goals, communication ability, and individual
needs, provided that their teaching remains consistent with Hamkke's
educational principles.

This distinction is important because the Framework expressly separates
Hamkke Required Standards, Teacher Professional Judgment, and
Learner-Specific Adaptation.

2. Availability and Lesson Assignments
The Teacher may provide Hamkke with the days and times during which they
are available to teach.

Providing availability does not guarantee that all available hours will
be assigned. Lesson assignments may depend on learner demand, scheduling
compatibility, learner needs, teacher suitability, and other reasonable
operational considerations.

Once a regular lesson schedule or individual lesson has been confirmed,
the Teacher is expected to honor that schedule unless a change has been
approved or an unexpected circumstance prevents the lesson from taking
place.

The Teacher should notify Hamkke as early as reasonably possible when
their regular availability changes.

Hamkke may modify, reassign, pause, or discontinue lesson assignments
when reasonably necessary because of enrollment changes, learner
requests, scheduling changes, teacher availability, or other operational
circumstances.

3. Outside Teaching and Other Work
The Teacher's teaching relationship with Hamkke is non-exclusive
unless Hamkke and the Teacher expressly agree otherwise in writing for a
particular role or arrangement.

The Teacher may teach for other companies, maintain independent
students, operate their own teaching activities, or engage in other
lawful employment or business activities.

Outside teaching or other work must not:

interfere with the Teacher's ability to fulfill lessons or
responsibilities already confirmed with Hamkke;
result in repeated lateness, cancellations, absences, or failure to
complete required Hamkke responsibilities;
involve the unauthorized solicitation, redirection, acceptance, or
movement of Hamkke-introduced learners into an outside teaching
arrangement;
involve the unauthorized use or disclosure of learner information,
confidential Hamkke information, proprietary Hamkke materials,
accounts, systems, or internal resources;
cause the Teacher to represent another company, service, or private
teaching activity as being affiliated with or endorsed by Hamkke
without authorization; or
otherwise create a material conflict with the Teacher's obligations
under this Agreement.
The Teacher is not required to disclose unrelated outside teaching,
employment, clients, or business activities merely because they exist.
However, the Teacher should promptly disclose a specific outside
arrangement if it creates or is reasonably likely to create a direct
conflict involving a Hamkke learner, confidential information, confirmed
Hamkke teaching responsibilities, or another obligation under this
Agreement.

Nothing in this Section prohibits the Teacher from generally competing
in the English-teaching market, advertising their own teaching services
to the public, or accepting learners who were not introduced to the
Teacher through Hamkke.

For clarity, the Teacher's uncommitted time remains their own. Once the
Teacher accepts or confirms a Hamkke lesson or regular schedule,
however, the Teacher is expected to fulfill that commitment in
accordance with this Agreement and applicable Hamkke policies.

4. Compensation and Rate Progression
The Teacher will be compensated according to the applicable teaching
rate recorded by Hamkke.

The standard teaching rates under this Agreement are:

Qualifying Teaching Hours 25-Minute Lesson 50-Minute Lesson

0--999 hours ₱125.00 ₱250.00
1,000--1,999 hours ₱137.50 ₱275.00
2,000--2,999 hours ₱150.00 ₱300.00
3,000--3,999 hours ₱162.50 ₱325.00
4,000+ hours ₱175.00 ₱350.00

For purposes of rate progression, qualifying teaching hours means
actual completed teaching time.

A student no-show, late cancellation, or another lesson that is payable
under Hamkke policy but during which teaching did not actually occur may
be included in compensation but does not count toward qualifying
teaching hours for rate progression.

When the Teacher reaches a qualifying teaching-hour threshold, the
corresponding higher teaching rate applies beginning with the next
lesson conducted after the threshold has been reached.

A lesson that causes the Teacher's accumulated qualifying teaching hours
to reach or exceed a threshold is compensated at the rate applicable
when that lesson began. A lesson will not be divided between two
compensation rates for purposes of rate progression.

Hamkke's system records of actual completed teaching time will be used
to determine accumulated qualifying teaching hours and the applicable
teaching rate. If the Teacher reasonably believes the recorded
qualifying hours are incorrect, the Teacher may request a review, and
Hamkke will correct any verified discrepancy.

Unless Hamkke expressly states otherwise in writing, the standard lesson
rate includes ordinary lesson preparation, routine attendance recording,
ordinary teacher notes, and Progress Reports or feedback customarily
required as part of the Teacher's assigned teaching responsibilities.
Work materially outside ordinary teaching and related documentation
responsibilities, including separately commissioned projects, will be
subject to any additional compensation or terms agreed when the work is
assigned.

Any change to the Teacher's compensation that is not already provided
for by the rate progression terms of this Agreement will be communicated
by Hamkke and will not be applied retroactively to lessons already
completed. A policy change that materially changes whether assigned
lessons are payable, or otherwise materially reduces compensation for
assigned teaching, will be treated as a material contractual change
rather than solely as an operational policy update.

5. Payroll and Payment
Hamkke calculates the Teacher's compensation based on lesson and
attendance records maintained in the Hamkke system.

The Teacher is responsible for accurately and promptly updating the
attendance status of lessons they conduct. These records are used by
Hamkke to determine completed and otherwise payable lessons for each
payroll period.

Payroll is processed twice each month according to Philippine Time
(PHT):

Lessons from the 1st through the 15th are normally paid on the
16th.
Lessons from the 16th through the last day of the month are
normally paid on the 1st day of the following month.
If a scheduled payment date falls on a day when the applicable banking
or payment service is unavailable, payment will normally be processed on
the next appropriate banking day, unless Hamkke processes it
earlier.

Payment will be made through a payment method approved by Hamkke. Hamkke
is responsible for any ordinary sending fee charged directly to Hamkke
for the approved payment method. Fees imposed solely by the Teacher's
receiving bank, wallet, payment provider, currency-conversion choice, or
withdrawal method are the Teacher's responsibility unless Hamkke
expressly agrees otherwise.

The Teacher is responsible for keeping payment information accurate and
current. If payment is delayed, rejected, or returned because the
Teacher supplied inaccurate, incomplete, expired, or otherwise unusable
payment information, Hamkke will reprocess the payment within a
reasonable time after corrected information is provided.

The Teacher is not required to separately submit a lesson count for
payroll unless requested by Hamkke to resolve an exceptional situation
or discrepancy.

If the Teacher believes that an attendance record, payable lesson,
qualifying teaching-hour total, or payment amount is incorrect, the
Teacher should notify Hamkke as soon as reasonably possible. Hamkke will
review the relevant system records and correct any verified discrepancy.

Any tax withholding, reporting, contribution, registration, or similar
obligation will be handled according to the legal nature of the teaching
relationship and applicable Philippine law. Nothing in this Section is
intended to waive or transfer an obligation that applicable law places
on Hamkke or the Teacher.

6. Lesson Attendance and Payable Lessons
A lesson that is successfully conducted is recorded as completed and is
payable according to the Teacher's applicable rate.

A lesson may also be considered payable when the Hamkke learner
attendance and cancellation policy in effect on the scheduled date of
the lesson provides that the lesson is counted despite the learner not
attending or completing the lesson. This may include applicable student
no-shows or late cancellations.

A lesson being payable does not necessarily mean that actual
teaching time occurred. Only actual completed teaching time counts
toward the qualifying teaching hours described in Section 4.

If a lesson begins but cannot be completed because of a learner's late
arrival, early departure, connectivity problem, technical issue, or
other learner-side circumstance, its attendance, payability, qualifying
teaching time, and any rescheduling or learner credit will be determined
under the applicable attendance policy and the actual circumstances
recorded for the lesson.

If a lesson begins but cannot be completed because of a Teacher-side
power interruption, internet failure, illness, emergency, significant
technical problem, or other Teacher-side circumstance, the Teacher must
accurately record and promptly report what occurred. Hamkke will
determine the appropriate operational treatment under the applicable
policy, including whether the lesson is rescheduled, credited, partially
or fully payable where applicable, and how much actual teaching time
counts toward qualifying teaching hours.

Where actual teaching occurs for only part of a lesson, only the
teaching time actually conducted counts toward qualifying teaching hours
unless an applicable Hamkke policy expressly provides another method of
recording qualifying teaching time. Payability and qualifying teaching
time are separate determinations.

Teachers are responsible for recording attendance accurately and must
not intentionally mark a lesson as completed, payable, cancelled, or
otherwise attended in a manner inconsistent with what actually occurred.

Hamkke may review attendance records when reasonably necessary to
resolve discrepancies or maintain accurate learner and payroll records.

7. Teacher Cancellations, Absences, and Unexpected Circumstances
The Teacher is expected to attend all confirmed lessons.

When the Teacher knows in advance that they cannot conduct a scheduled
lesson, they should notify Hamkke as early as reasonably possible so
appropriate arrangements can be made.

Unexpected circumstances may include illness, emergencies, power
interruptions, internet failures, significant technical problems, or
other circumstances reasonably outside the Teacher's control.

When such circumstances occur, the Teacher should communicate with
Hamkke as soon as reasonably possible.

Depending on the circumstances, Hamkke may arrange a rescheduled lesson,
learner credit, substitute teacher where appropriate, or another
reasonable operational solution.

A genuine unexpected circumstance will not automatically be treated in
the same manner as an unexplained absence.

However, repeated cancellations, repeated lateness, unexplained
absences, failure to communicate, or an ongoing inability to fulfill
confirmed teaching schedules may result in changes to lesson assignments
or termination of the teaching relationship.

8. Professional Conduct and Learner Relationships
The Teacher agrees to maintain respectful, appropriate, and professional
relationships with learners, parents or guardians, other teachers, and
Hamkke.

The Teacher must maintain appropriate professional boundaries,
particularly when teaching or communicating with minors.

Communication with learners and parents should occur through
Hamkke-approved channels or through other channels specifically
authorized by Hamkke when necessary for lesson delivery.

The Teacher must not use the teaching relationship to pressure learners
or their families into purchasing unrelated products or services.

For purposes of this Section, a Hamkke-introduced learner means a
learner, parent, guardian, or household whose teaching relationship or
contact with the Teacher arose through Hamkke, excluding a person with
whom the Teacher can reasonably demonstrate a pre-existing independent
teaching or personal relationship before the Hamkke introduction.

While actively teaching through Hamkke, the Teacher must not, without
Hamkke's prior written approval, directly or indirectly solicit,
recruit, redirect, arrange, accept, or facilitate private lessons or
another teaching arrangement outside Hamkke with a Hamkke-introduced
learner.

This restriction applies whether the outside arrangement is first
proposed by the Teacher, the learner, a parent or guardian, or another
person acting on their behalf. If a Hamkke-introduced learner approaches
the Teacher about moving lessons outside Hamkke, the Teacher should
direct the learner to Hamkke and promptly disclose the approach to
Hamkke.

For six (6) months after the Teacher's teaching relationship with
Hamkke ends, the Teacher must not, without Hamkke's prior written
approval, directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, redirect, arrange,
accept, or facilitate private lessons or another competing teaching
arrangement outside Hamkke with a Hamkke-introduced learner whom the
Teacher taught or had material teaching-related contact with during the
final twelve (12) months of the relationship.

This restriction applies whether the outside arrangement is first
proposed by the Teacher, the learner, a parent or guardian, or another
person acting on their behalf.

The post-relationship restriction does not prohibit general advertising
that is not specifically directed at Hamkke learners, nor does it
prevent the Teacher from teaching a person with whom the Teacher had a
demonstrable independent relationship before that person's introduction
through Hamkke.

This Section is intended to protect Hamkke's existing learner
relationships and is not intended to prevent the Teacher from generally
working as an English teacher or providing teaching services to other
learners.

A material violation of this Section may constitute a serious breach of
this Agreement.

9. Confidentiality, Privacy, and Learner Information
The Teacher may receive access to learner information that is necessary
for teaching and educational continuity.

This information may include names, contact or guardian information,
schedules, lesson history, teacher notes, learning goals, educational
records, and information voluntarily shared during lessons.

The Teacher agrees to access, process, and use learner information only
for legitimate and authorized Hamkke teaching or administrative
purposes, in accordance with Hamkke instructions, applicable privacy
procedures, and applicable law.

The Teacher must not:

disclose learner information to unauthorized persons;
share account credentials;
allow another person to access learner information through the
Teacher's account;
unnecessarily copy, export, photograph, screenshot, or download
learner records;
retain learner information for personal purposes;
use learner information for purposes unrelated to authorized Hamkke
services; or
transfer learner or Hamkke information to an unauthorized
third-party service.
The Teacher must not upload, submit, disclose, or process identifiable
learner information, lesson transcripts, recordings, screenshots,
messages, teacher notes, educational records, or confidential Hamkke
information through a third-party artificial intelligence,
transcription, recording, storage, analytics, or similar service unless
Hamkke has authorized that service and its use for the relevant purpose.

Personal information may naturally arise during conversational lessons.
Teachers should exercise discretion and should not unnecessarily record
private information simply because it was mentioned during conversation.
Records should preserve only information that meaningfully and
appropriately helps Hamkke understand and support the learner.

The Teacher must take reasonable steps to protect learner and Hamkke
information accessed through the Teacher's devices or accounts,
including protecting login credentials, preventing unauthorized viewing
or access, and following Hamkke's applicable account and
information-security procedures.

The Teacher should promptly notify Hamkke if they become aware of
unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, compromised credentials,
lost or compromised devices containing Hamkke information, or another
incident that may affect learner or Hamkke information.

Upon Hamkke's reasonable request, or when the teaching relationship
ends, the Teacher must return or securely delete learner personal
information and confidential Hamkke information held outside authorized
Hamkke systems, including copies, screenshots, exports, and locally
stored files, unless retention is required by applicable law. Where
lawful retention is required, the information must remain protected and
may be used only for the legally permitted purpose.

The Teacher's confidentiality obligations concerning non-public learner
and Hamkke information continue after the teaching relationship ends.

10. Lesson Recordings, Screenshots, and Public Use
The Teacher must not independently record lessons or take or retain
screenshots except for an authorized Hamkke teaching, safety, support,
or administrative purpose and in accordance with applicable Hamkke
procedures.

The Teacher must not publish, distribute, or publicly share a learner's
image, voice, name, messages, work, lesson content, or other
identifiable information without documented authorization obtained
through a process approved by Hamkke and any additional consent
required by applicable law or Hamkke policy.

A learner's or parent's informal verbal permission, participation in a
lesson, or voluntary sharing of material during a lesson does not by
itself authorize the Teacher to use that material for a separate
purpose.

Participation in a Hamkke lesson does not by itself constitute
permission for the Teacher to use lesson content for social media,
advertising, portfolios, promotional materials, training materials, or
other public purposes.

Any authorized recording, screenshot, or use of learner material must
follow applicable Hamkke privacy, retention, security, and consent
procedures.

11. Learner Records, Notes, and Progress Reports
Where required by Hamkke systems or procedures, the Teacher agrees to
maintain lesson records, observations, feedback, and Progress Report
information reasonably necessary for educational continuity.

Teacher documentation should be:

concise;
factual;
educationally relevant;
based on actual observation;
useful for future lessons; and
written professionally and respectfully.
The Teaching Framework specifically establishes these qualities for
Hamkke teacher documentation.

Teachers should distinguish between an observation and a claim of
learner progress.

An isolated performance should not automatically be described as
improvement. Statements regarding development should be based on
reasonable evidence across lessons whenever possible.

The Framework makes the same distinction: an observation describes what
happened, while a progress statement requires evidence across time.

Records created or maintained within Hamkke's systems form part of
Hamkke's educational records and should remain available to Hamkke for
legitimate educational and operational purposes.

Teachers should record only information reasonably relevant to teaching
and learner support and should avoid unnecessary personal judgments or
speculation.

12. Teaching Materials and Intellectual Property
Materials, curriculum, lesson resources, templates, internal documents,
training materials, systems, branding, and other content supplied by
Hamkke remain the property of Hamkke or their respective rights holders,
as applicable.

The Teacher may use Hamkke-provided materials as reasonably necessary to
perform authorized teaching responsibilities.

The Teacher must not sell, redistribute, publicly publish, reproduce for
unrelated commercial purposes, or otherwise commercially exploit
proprietary Hamkke materials without authorization.

Materials independently created and owned by the Teacher before their
use with Hamkke remain the Teacher's property unless otherwise agreed in
writing.

The Teacher may use appropriate teacher-created or third-party teaching
resources where permitted by Hamkke and where their use remains
consistent with applicable teaching standards. The Teacher must not
knowingly upload, distribute, reproduce, or use third-party materials
through Hamkke in a manner that violates applicable copyright,
licensing, or other intellectual-property restrictions.

If Hamkke specifically commissions the Teacher to create proprietary
curriculum, teaching materials, templates, training resources, media, or
other original work outside the Teacher's ordinary teaching
responsibilities, the ownership, permitted use, compensation where
applicable, and any required intellectual-property assignment or license
must be established in writing when the work is commissioned. Unless
such written terms are agreed, this Agreement alone does not transfer
ownership to Hamkke of independently created Teacher materials.

Nothing in this Section prevents the Teacher from using general teaching
knowledge, skills, methods, or experience that do not disclose Hamkke
confidential information or reproduce proprietary Hamkke materials.

13. Hamkke Policies and Teaching Standards
The Teacher agrees to comply with applicable Hamkke policies,
procedures, and internal standards reasonably related to their teaching
responsibilities.

These may include policies concerning:

teaching and lesson delivery;
scheduling and attendance;
learner communication;
learner records;
confidentiality and privacy;
learner safety and professional boundaries;
feedback and Progress Reports;
platform and account use; and
teaching quality and educational standards.
The Hamkke Teaching Framework forms part of Hamkke's internal
teaching standards.

The Framework is intended to maintain a recognizable educational
experience while allowing teachers to exercise professional judgment
rather than requiring identical teaching styles.

Hamkke may develop and revise its policies, procedures, teaching
standards, and Teaching Framework as its services and educational
systems develop.

Purely administrative or implementation changes that do not alter a
Teacher's rights, obligations, compensation, teaching standards, or
other terms governing the teaching relationship may be communicated
without issuing a new Teacher Agreement version.

However, if Hamkke changes a policy, teaching standard, Teaching
Framework requirement, or other incorporated standard in a manner that
changes or adds to the terms, responsibilities, rights, or standards
governing the Teacher, the change will be incorporated into a new
Teacher Agreement version or amendment and presented to the Teacher in
accordance with Section 15.

An already accepted agreement will not be silently changed through a
later policy, procedure, Teaching Framework revision, portal
instruction, or internal standard.

14. Ending the Teaching Relationship
Either Hamkke or the Teacher may end the teaching relationship, subject
to applicable law and this Agreement.

For an ordinary ending of the relationship that does not involve serious
breach, safety concerns, or circumstances making advance notice
unreasonable, the Teacher should provide at least fourteen (14)
calendar days' written notice where reasonably possible so Hamkke can
manage existing learner schedules and continuity of lessons.

Similarly, where reasonably possible and subject to applicable law,
Hamkke should provide reasonable written notice of the ending of regular
teaching assignments and identify the effective date and any remaining
responsibilities.

A shorter notice period may be accepted by Hamkke, and either party may
be unable to provide the ordinary notice because of emergency, serious
illness, safety concerns, material breach, or another circumstance
reasonably making continued performance impracticable.

Hamkke may immediately suspend lesson assignments or end the teaching
relationship when reasonably necessary in cases involving serious
misconduct, learner safety concerns, intentional misuse of learner
information or Hamkke systems, unauthorized disclosure of confidential
information, material unauthorized solicitation or redirection of Hamkke
learners, falsification of attendance or payment-related records, fraud,
intentional misuse of Hamkke accounts or systems, or another serious
breach of this Agreement.

Whether another breach is sufficiently serious to justify immediate
suspension or ending may take into account the nature and intent of the
conduct, whether it is repeated, its effect on learner safety or
privacy, falsification or dishonesty, material harm or risk to learners
or Hamkke, and whether the breach can reasonably be corrected.

Repeated unexplained absences combined with failure to respond to
reasonable Hamkke attempts to contact the Teacher may be treated as an
indication that the Teacher is no longer available to fulfill confirmed
teaching assignments. Hamkke may reassign affected learners immediately
and, after reasonable attempts to obtain clarification, may end the
teaching relationship, subject to applicable law.

When the teaching relationship ends, the Teacher must:

complete any reasonably required outstanding attendance or learner
records;
stop accessing Hamkke systems when authorization ends;
return or securely delete learner and confidential Hamkke
information as required by Section 9;
discontinue unauthorized use of proprietary Hamkke materials; and
comply with any obligations under this Agreement that expressly
continue after the relationship ends.
Compensation properly earned for lessons already completed or otherwise
payable before the effective end of the teaching relationship remains
payable according to the applicable payroll arrangements.

15. Changes to This Agreement
Each Teacher Agreement is identified by a version number.

Once the Teacher accepts a version of this Agreement, that accepted
version will be preserved as the agreement accepted by the Teacher.
Hamkke will not silently modify, replace, or overwrite the terms of an
agreement version that has already been accepted.

If Hamkke changes a contractual term, teacher-governing policy, teaching
standard, Teaching Framework requirement, or other incorporated
requirement in a manner intended to change or add to the terms,
responsibilities, rights, or standards governing the Teacher, Hamkke
will issue a new agreement version or amendment identifying the updated
terms.

The new version or amendment will be presented to the Teacher for review
and digital acceptance before the changed terms become applicable to the
Teacher, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

Until the Teacher accepts the new version, the Teacher's previously
accepted agreement remains the recorded agreement governing the existing
teaching relationship, subject to applicable law.

Unless the new version expressly specifies a later effective date, an
accepted new agreement version becomes effective when the Teacher
digitally accepts it.

Acceptance of a new agreement version does not retroactively change
compensation, obligations, lesson records, or other matters already
completed under an earlier version unless expressly agreed by both
parties or required by applicable law.

Hamkke will maintain a record of each agreement version accepted by the
Teacher, including its version number and acceptance date.

16. General Terms
This Agreement, together with applicable Hamkke policies and standards
referenced by it, records the terms and expectations governing the
Teacher's teaching relationship with Hamkke.

Order of precedence. If this Agreement conflicts with an operational
policy, procedure, Teaching Framework provision, portal instruction, or
other internal standard, this Agreement controls unless a later
agreement or amendment accepted by the Teacher expressly states
otherwise. A specific written compensation term agreed for a particular
assignment controls over a conflicting general operational instruction
for that assignment.

Applicable law. This Agreement and the parties' rights and
obligations are subject to applicable laws and regulations of the
Republic of the Philippines. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to
waive a right, protection, obligation, or remedy that cannot lawfully be
waived.

Nature of relationship. The legal nature of the relationship between
Hamkke and the Teacher will be determined by the parties' actual
arrangement and applicable law. No label, policy, or provision of this
Agreement is intended to remove statutory rights or obligations that
apply to the relationship.

If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remaining provisions should continue to apply to the
extent permitted.

A failure by either Hamkke or the Teacher to immediately enforce a
provision does not automatically waive that provision.

Important communications relating to this Agreement may be delivered
through the Teacher Portal, the Teacher's registered email address, or
another official communication channel designated by Hamkke.

Nothing in this Agreement should be interpreted as requiring a teacher
to follow a fixed teaching script where the Hamkke Teaching Framework
expressly permits appropriate professional judgment and learner-specific
adaptation.
